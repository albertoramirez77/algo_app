  ECONOMIC REASON, not a performance reason. QG's minimum
  price increment is 0.005 per unit, worth $12.50, set
  when the underlying traded far higher. On a $7,520
  notional that single tick is 16.6bp - the universe median
  tick is 2.23bp. The increment was never rescaled
  as the underlying declined.

  The last column is the decisive one: cost as a share of ONE MONTH of
  that instrument's own volatility. A genuinely volatile contract can
  support a wider spread, so this adjusts for opportunity. QG
  costs 1.65% of a month's volatility against a median
  of 0.58% - it remains an outlier after the
  adjustment, which is what makes the case decisive rather than merely
  arithmetic.
